About Sahra

Ever searched TikTok for a party tonight?

Or wanted to host one, and spent the week answering DMs, checking payment screenshots and keeping names in your notes? Sahra puts it in one place. Guests find the party, purchase a ticket and get a QR code. Organisers approve, check payments and run the door from their phone.

Why Sahra

Parties shouldn't live in your DMs.

One place to find them

Upcoming parties are listed with their date, price and whether tickets are still open. No scrolling through stories to find a flyer.

Know where you stand

After you request, your ticket page shows if you are waiting, approved or turned down, and the address when the organiser shares it.

Your QR is your ticket

Show it at the door. It works once, so a screenshot passed around cannot get anyone else in.

For organisers

Host without the spreadsheet.

Approve in one tap

Each request is a card with the guest's details and payment screenshot. Approve and send the QR together.

Your tickets, your rules

Ticket types with their own prices, places and sales dates, and your own sign-up questions.

The address when you choose

Keep the location private and share it with approved guests at the time you pick.

The door on any phone

Your door team scans with their phone camera. A used ticket shows red, and if the scan cannot be checked it says so instead of letting anyone through.

See the night at a glance

How many are approved, how many are waiting, the money you should have received and who is inside.

Your guest list

Download every request and ticket as a spreadsheet whenever you need it.

How hosting works

From first message to the door.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Tell us about your party on WhatsApp, email or the contact form.

  2. 02

    We set you up

    Sahra is by invitation. We create your party and invite your Google account, so there is no password to remember.

  3. 03

    Share your link

    Add your tickets and questions, then share the party link. Requests come straight to you.

  4. 04

    Run the night

    Approve guests, send their QR codes and scan them in at the door.

اعرف أين وصل طلبك

رمز QR هو تذكرتك
